# Phase 4 sample — Copy-safe department art

## Accepted composition rule

- Products occupy the right half of the frame.
- The left 42–46% remains quiet and object-free for HTML copy.
- Text may sit in the reserved copy zone, but never over the product group.
- Smaller placements can alternate direction later, provided they preserve the same separation.
- Photography uses warm-white studio space, soft contact shadows, one department accent, and no embedded text, logos, packaging, or watermarks.

## Generation

Built-in image generation was used. Both prompts specified premium photorealistic product still lifes, strict left-copy/right-products framing, warm-white studio backgrounds, one restrained department accent, crop safety for 16:10 and 4:3 placements, and explicit exclusions for text, logos, trademarks, watermarks, people, retailer packaging, floating objects, cutout halos, and glossy CGI rendering.

- Electronics subject set: laptop, headphones, controller, and smartphone; pale aqua accent.
- Fashion subject set: burnt-orange jacket, charcoal handbag, neutral knitwear, and shoes; muted coral accent.
