# Research register

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-21

This register separates authoritative external requirements/guidance,
independent usability research, competitor observations, African-market
research, and local Alkemart documents. Competitor pages are inspiration and
market evidence, not instructions or specifications.

## African and Ghanaian market evidence

- GSMA, [E-commerce in Africa: Unleashing the Opportunities for
  MSMEs](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/E-CommerceInAfrica_R_WebSingles.pdf).
  Evidence on social commerce, informal MSMEs, payments, delivery, and market
  participation across Ghana and five other African markets.
- GSMA, [What challenges do African MSMEs face in adopting
  e-commerce?](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/blog/what-challenges-do-african-msmes-face-in-adopting-e-commerce/).
- IFC, [Women and e-commerce in
  Africa](https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2021/women-and-ecommerce-africa).
- IFC, [Venture Capital and the Rise of Africa's Tech
  Startups](https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2025/venture-capital-and-the-rise-of-africas-tech-startups).
- World Bank, [Boosting small businesses through e-commerce and integration
  into value chains](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/psd/boosting-small-businesses-through-e-commerce-and-integration-value-chains).
- World Bank, [Africa's Pulse — digital adoption by informal
  businesses](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/908441617255950693/pdf/Africa-s-Pulse.pdf).
- UN Trade and Development, [Leveraging digital technologies for MSME
  integration](https://unctad.org/publication/leveraging-digital-technologies-msmes-integration-regional-value-and-supply-chains).

in Ghana.
