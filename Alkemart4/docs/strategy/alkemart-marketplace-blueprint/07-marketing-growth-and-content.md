# 07 — Marketing, growth, and content

## Marketing thesis

Alkemart should market confident buying decisions and credible shops, not an
undifferentiated volume of products.

Core messages:

- Compare trusted offers for the product you want.
- Shop credible stores in one polished marketplace.
- Understand total cost and delivery before checkout.
- Open and grow a professional digital shop.

## Campaign families

### Demand capture

- Brand/model and product-type landing experiences.
- Budget missions: “phones under GH₵2,000.”
- Problem missions: “set up a student room.”
- Local availability and pickup.
- Category buying guides.

### Demand creation

- Shop spotlights and founder stories.
- Curated edits built around lifestyle moments.
- Seasonal visual campaigns.
- New brand/store launches.
- Marketplace price and availability insights based on real aggregate data.

### Trust campaigns

- How seller verification works.
- How comparisons are matched.
- Payment and delivery education.
- Returns and dispute protection.
- Anti-scam education using Ghanaian context.

### Seller growth

- Store setup and catalog-quality education.
- Product photography and merchandising guidance.
- Inventory, fulfillment, and customer-service playbooks.
- Category opportunity insights.

## Lifecycle journeys

Only send messages with a clear buyer or seller benefit:

- Account/order transactional messages.
- Cart reminder after measured intent, with conservative frequency.
- Saved-product price or availability change.
- Saved-search/new matching product.
- Back-in-stock.
- Delivery and pickup progress.
- Verified-purchase review request.
- Followed-shop new collection, later and opt-in.
- Seller low-stock, stale-price, order SLA, payout, and policy alerts.

Notification preferences are channel-specific. Transactional and promotional
messages remain distinct.

## Content pillars

### Buying and comparison guides

Searchable answers for specifications, sizing, compatibility, quality, and
budget decisions. These connect naturally to category and product pages.

### Ghana shopping operations

MoMo/COD, digital addresses, pickup, regional delivery, returns, warranties,
and scam avoidance.

### Product ownership

Setup, care, maintenance, accessories, troubleshooting, and replacement.

### Seller education

How to build a credible catalog, photograph products, manage stock, fulfill,
price responsibly, and use collections/campaigns.

## Content scoring

Prioritize ideas using:

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Customer impact | 40% |
| Content-market fit | 30% |
| Search potential | 20% |
| Resource requirement | 10% |

Start with consideration-stage content close to commerce, then build broad
awareness authority. Every page must be searchable, shareable, or both.

## Product marketing data contract

Campaigns require stable product IDs, price/availability freshness, explicit
creative slots, promotion IDs, and source attribution. Track impressions,
selections, PDP engagement, offer selection, add-to-cart, checkout, purchase,
refund, and delivered order.

## Growth loops

```text
better catalog data
→ better discovery
→ more qualified buyers
→ more seller demand
→ better offer coverage
→ stronger comparison
```

```text
delivered order
→ verified review
→ stronger product/seller trust
→ higher conversion
→ more delivered orders
```

```text
seller analytics
→ improved stock/content/fulfillment
→ better buyer experience
→ more seller revenue
→ seller retention and assortment
```

## Cautions

- Do not build growth around permanent discounting.
- Do not use false countdowns, fake scarcity, or misleading compare-at prices.
- Do not let sponsored placement masquerade as organic ranking.
- Do not send promotions before preference/consent infrastructure exists.
- Do not publish thin AI-generated category pages.
- Do not optimize clicks while ignoring delivered orders, returns, complaints,
  and seller economics.
